Blood Bridge

In India, urgent blood requests spread as unverified WhatsApp forwards, and scammers ask for fake “processing fees”. Blood Bridge verifies the request, routes it to nearby blood banks and compatible opt-in donors in Bengaluru, and tracks it until it's met.

Live on ASI:One, with a safe demo mode that never alerts real donors.
